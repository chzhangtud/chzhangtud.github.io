Review multi-view geometry through eleven Sceaux Castle photographs. Part one develops projection, epipolar constraints, and triangulation; part two connects tracks, PnP, and bundle adjustment; part three derives plane-induced warps for dense matching, consistency, and fusion. Essential equations remain in a continuous narrative, with longer derivations available to expand. Image correspondences, synthetic BA, real-photo plane sweeping, and 3D geometry appear alongside the principles they illustrate.
